# Expression Guardrails Template

Use this file to protect terms, expressions, and argument boundaries that should not be rewritten casually.

## Terms To Keep

| Term | Meaning | Rewrite allowed? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI workflow | AI connected to a repeatable process | no | Keep if it is a core concept. |
| personal operating system | A person's repeatable knowledge/work loop | limited | Can explain, not replace. |

## Claims To Avoid

- Do not claim a tool can replace an entire profession without evidence.
- Do not present personal judgment as verified industry fact.
- Do not turn operational insight into motivational slogans.

## Tone Guardrails

- Avoid exaggerated certainty.
- Avoid generic trend-chasing language.
- Prefer concrete workflow descriptions.
