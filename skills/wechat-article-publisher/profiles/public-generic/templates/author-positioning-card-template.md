# Author Positioning Card Template

Use this card before drafting to decide whether author identity should appear in the article.

## Decision

- article_topic:
- is_judgment_heavy: true/false
- needs_author_credibility: true/false
- best_identity_anchor:
- use_depth: none / light / medium / central

## Placement Options

- opening:
- middle_explanation:
- conclusion:

## Rule

If the article makes a strong judgment about an industry, product, management decision, or workflow shift, add at least one light identity anchor unless there is a reason not to.

## Fake Example

- best_identity_anchor: I have watched enterprise tools move from expensive deployment projects to daily workflow utilities.
- use_depth: light
- placement: middle_explanation
