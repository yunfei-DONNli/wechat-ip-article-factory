# Revision Rules Template

Use this file to accumulate what the human author changes when revising AI drafts.

## Keep

- Strong opening judgment when supported by context.
- Short paragraphs with one clear job.
- Concrete workflow details.

## Avoid

- Long abstract introductions.
- Repeating the title as a section quote.
- Generic AI enthusiasm without personal anchor.

## Watch

- Screenshot descriptions must match the actual image.
- Article index should explain the structure, not tease vague viewpoints.

## Sample Revision Pattern

| AI draft issue | Human revision | Rule learned |
|---|---|---|
| "AI is changing everything" | "AI is not changing work by itself; workflow ownership is changing." | Replace generic claims with operational judgment. |
