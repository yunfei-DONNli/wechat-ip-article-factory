# Author Identity Template

Use this file to define why the author is qualified to make judgment-heavy claims. Do not include private identity data unless this profile is private.

## Required Fields

- author_name:
- public_role:
- industry_domain:
- years_of_experience:
- firsthand_context:
- credibility_boundary:

## Identity Summary

Write 3-5 sentences describing the author's public-facing identity.

Example:

> I have worked for more than ten years in enterprise software and operations. My writing focuses on how AI changes team workflows, not on model hype. I only make strong claims when they connect to my own work experience or observable industry changes.

## Use In Articles

Use this identity when the article needs to answer:

- Why can this author say this?
- What has the author personally seen?
- Where should the author avoid overclaiming?
