# Publish Closeout Checklist

## Before Draft Creation

- title locked
- title synced
- article HTML ready
- publish package ready
- cover ready
- body-image decision complete
- fixed links complete

## After Draft Creation

- publish notes updated
- control page updated
- inbox/source note updated if needed

## After Formal Publish

- public article link recorded
- final content archived to `已发布/`
- published link index updated
