# Title Generation

## Goal

Generate WeChat-native titles that are faithful, clear, and attractive.

## Default Output

- 3-5 candidate titles
- 1 recommended title
- keep / tweak / rewrite judgment
- reference sources used

## Good Title Traits

- faithful to the article's core judgment
- easy to scan on mobile
- contains tension, judgment, or benefit
- not clickbait

## Sync Rule

Once selected, the title must be synced to article draft, publish version, HTML output, publish package, and draft-box title.
